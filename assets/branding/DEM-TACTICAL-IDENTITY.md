# DemoStrike .DEM identity

The mark is an angular demo-file badge for DemoStrike's CS2 replay analysis and capture workflow. The folded corner identifies a file; `.DEM` names the demo-file format. Two rewind cutouts suggest replay and review, while the counter inside the D carries a crosshair motif.

## Original vector source

The badge and separate DEMOSTRIKE wordmark were created for DemoStrike on 21 October 2026 from original SVG path geometry. The lettering is custom geometry, not a font asset. No stock mark, glyph library, raster tracing or edited raster artwork is used. PNG exports are direct renders of the SVG masters, and the ICO contains those PNG payloads.

The square app badge does not include the separate DEMOSTRIKE wordmark or a caption.

## Variants and use

| Asset | Intended use |
|---|---|
| `demostrike-dem-tactical.svg` | Native icon master: yellow frame, warm-ivory lettering and a dark inset field. Use where the surrounding surface may be light or dark. The exterior and rewind cutouts remain transparent. |
| `demostrike-dem-tactical.png` | Direct 512 px native-icon render for application APIs that consume PNG. |
| `demostrike-dem-tactical.ico` | Windows icon container with exact 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px PNG payloads. |
| `demostrike-dem-tactical-dark.svg` | Open transparent yellow/ivory variant for a controlled dark surface. |
| `demostrike-dem-tactical-light.svg` | Open transparent deep-gold/ink variant for a controlled light surface. |
| `demostrike-dem-tactical-mono.svg` | Single-ink artwork for a suitable contrasting surface. |
| `demostrike-header-dark.svg`, `demostrike-header-light.svg` | Combined badge and separate wordmark for README mastheads or other larger headers. Select the variant for its surrounding surface. |
| `demostrike-wordmark-dark.svg`, `demostrike-wordmark-light.svg` | Standalone custom lettering for larger layouts. Keep it separate from small window and taskbar icons. |

The native icon master supplies its own dark contrast field. The open variants do not: their interior remains transparent and depends on the chosen background. The palette is yellow `#FFDC00`, warm ivory `#FFF6D4`, ink `#111613` and deep gold `#846500`.

## Rendering and small-size limits

The repository supplies a 512 px native PNG and seven PNG payloads inside the ICO. Production checks covered direct SVG renders at 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256, 512 and 1024 px, including centered alpha bounds, clear exterior corners, retained transparent cutouts and exact ICO payload matches. The 16 px export is a silhouette and contrast proof; it makes no claim that the `.DEM` lettering or crosshair detail is readable at that size.

Use the badge with ordinary adjacent application text in a compact title bar. Use the separate header or wordmark only when the layout gives the lettering enough space.

## Preservation and verification scope

This identity is additive. Original animal artwork, earlier abstract geometric assets, publisher artwork and their provenance remain preserved at their existing paths. New assets use their own names.

These records describe artwork and asset rendering. They do not establish native application appearance, taskbar behavior, interactive Room acceptance, a release or deployment. The identity update does not call for changes to application behavior, dependency pins, security settings or bundle activation.
